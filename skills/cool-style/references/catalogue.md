# Catalogue

The strongest studied sites, grouped by what they are. Each kind lists the exemplars (★) first, then every other site rated 5/5 for distinctiveness. `[dead]`, `[changed]` and `[blocked]` mark sites that no longer look as they did when saved — their move is still worth knowing. Full notes for all 916: `data/sites.json`, or `node scripts/examples.mjs <word>`.
