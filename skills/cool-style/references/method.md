# Where this comes from

This guide is built from a close look at 916 independent websites — personal sites, net art, type foundries, archives, studios, small tools, journals — saved between 2013 and 2026 by people who collect the web's best corners, and a written note on each one. Read this when you need to know how sure a claim is.

## What each site got

A note in a fixed format: what it is; its type, colour, layout and imagery in concrete terms; its behaviour; *the one signature move*; why it works; one instruction you could steal tomorrow; 2–5 named moves; 5–12 tags from a controlled vocabulary; and ratings for distinctiveness (1–5) and craft (1–5). 108 sites (about 1 in 8) were flagged *exemplars* — teach-from-this quality. All the notes are in `data/sites.json`; `scripts/examples.mjs` searches them.

## How sure to be

- **Measured figures** (typefaces, colours, sizes, page weight, CSS and script features) describe the 755 sites still online in October 2026, as they stood then. They miss anything a site only builds after it loads, and page weight leaves out images and third-party scripts.
- **161 sites are gone, walled or redesigned** since they were saved. Their notes describe them as they were, because the reason they were worth keeping still stands; `examples.mjs --live` hides them.
- **Tags and ratings are judgements.** Treat a tag count as "roughly how common", not a measurement.
- **Percentages** are of the 755 live sites unless a line says otherwise.
